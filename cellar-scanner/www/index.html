Camera

Tin barcode (UPC / EAN)

Pick the rear camera in the list when you can (often labeled “back” or “environment”). Scanning reads the same live preview as above — align the barcode in the gold frame, hold steady, and tap Scan barcode. Many tins use a standard UPC/EAN on the bottom or side.

Manufacture / date sticker

Frame the printed date or batch sticker, then capture. OCR is best in good light; tap a suggested date or type your own.
