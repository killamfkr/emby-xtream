Cellar Scanner

Tin intake · camera-first cellar logging

Scan a UPC/EAN, read a date sticker, then export CSV for Tobacco Cellar or any sheet. UI is tuned like modern pipe apps (e.g. ThePiper) — this project is not affiliated; it only borrows the “open camera, grab a tin” flow.

Scan a tin

Opens a fullscreen viewfinder on your phone. We use the browser’s native barcode engine when available (fast on Chrome/Android), then fall back to ZXing on other browsers.

Barcode & lookup

After a read we query Open Food Facts to pre-fill empty brand / blend / size when a grocery listing exists — many pipe tins are missing; verify or edit. If this UPC is already in your list below, matching rows appear here so you can reuse your own blend names.

Manufacture / date sticker

Frame the printed date or batch sticker, then capture. OCR likes bright, even light; tap a suggestion or type your own.
